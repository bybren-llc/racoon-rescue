# WTFB Logline Development: Racoon Rescue V2

## WTFB Hook Structure

Per WTFB methodology, a compelling logline contains:
- **Characters**: Who is the protagonist? What defines them?
- **Conflict**: What opposes them? What's at stake?
- **Setting**: Where and when? What world?
- **Action**: What do they DO? The active choice?

---

## Logline Variations

### Version 1: The Action Hook
**When a drunk Navy petty officer uses an unconscious raccoon to bypass his court-ordered breathalyzer, a simple drive home becomes a violent, viral disaster that destroys his career and forces him to finally stop running from himself.**

### Version 2: The Character Hook
**A decorated sailor who's spent his life refusing help makes one desperate, absurd choice—using a wild animal to outsmart a breathalyzer—and learns that the illusion of control is the most dangerous drug of all.**

### Version 3: The Ironic Hook
**He survived combat deployments and a failed marriage. But it's a 30-pound raccoon that finally brings him to his knees.**

### Version 4: The Stakes Hook
**One unconscious raccoon. One court-ordered breathalyzer. One Navy career about to end in the most public, humiliating way possible—all because a man couldn't admit he needed a ride home.**

### Version 5: The Theme Hook
**A dark comedy about the moment the universe calls bullshit on our self-reliance mythology—delivered by a garbage-eating mammal with a mean right hook.**

---

## Selected Logline

**THE WINNER: Version 1 (Action Hook) - refined:**

> When a drunk Navy petty officer uses an unconscious raccoon to bypass his court-ordered breathalyzer, a simple drive home becomes a violent, viral disaster that forces him to finally stop fighting and surrender.

**Why this works:**
- Clear protagonist (Navy petty officer)
- Absurd but specific action (raccoon + breathalyzer)
- Stakes implied (court-ordered = legal trouble)
- Arc suggested (fighting → surrender)
- Tone established (violent, viral = dark comedy)

## Length Variants

### 25-Word Version
> A drunk sailor's desperate scheme to bypass his breathalyzer using an unconscious raccoon goes catastrophically wrong, ending his career and forcing his first honest moment.

### 50-Word Version
> When Danny Vega, a Navy petty officer with a court-ordered breathalyzer, discovers an unconscious raccoon in a parking lot at 2 AM, he sees a solution to all his problems. But the raccoon wakes up mid-drive, and what follows destroys his truck, his career, and finally—mercifully—his illusion of control.

### TV Guide One-Liner
> A drunk sailor + an unconscious raccoon + a breathalyzer = the worst night of everyone's life. *Fargo* meets *Reno 911*.

## The Dramatic Question

**External:** Will Danny get away with his terrible plan?
**Internal:** Will Danny finally admit he can't do this alone?

The logline must imply both questions are answered: NO and YES.
